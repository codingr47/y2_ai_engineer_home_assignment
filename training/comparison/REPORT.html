Joint comparison: E5 vs TF-IDF vs fusion, 77 fields vs 70 slots

Generated from training/comparison/REPORT.md by report_html.py.

Research question. Does character/word-level TF-IDF carry information that the frozen E5 sentence embeddings lose, and does combining the two improve complete slot detection?

Short answer.

  • Yes on both counts. TF-IDF beats E5 by +14.2 points of slot exact-set accuracy on test (95% CI +11.9 to +16.5). Fusion adds another +3.4 over TF-IDF (CI +1.8 to +5.0).
  • Fusion (Exp3) gives the best accuracy and the best confidence ranking for an LLM fallback. TF-IDF (Exp2) is the low-latency option: 3.4 points behind, about 6× faster per query, and it needs no 493 MB encoder.

3. What each representation contributes

Per slot (val_eval, dev; results/plots/slot_delta.png):

  • TF-IDF vs E5: 44 slots improve, 18 regress. Gains are on surface cues E5 blurs: off-list brand מותג +0.35, ריהוט +0.35, בעלות_מקרקעין +0.23, גודל_גלגל +0.17, יד_שנייה אזור +0.16, מצבי_עסקה +0.15. Losses are on numeric/year concepts often written as words: שנות_ייצור −0.24, ממ״ד −0.14, שנת_ייצור −0.11, מ״ר_בנוי⇄טווח_מ״ר −0.11.
  • Fusion vs TF-IDF: 31 improve, 18 regress (mostly small). Fusion recovers the numeric/year slots (שנות_ייצור +0.15, ממ״ד +0.14, יד_שנייה מחיר +0.11). Fusion vs E5: 46 improve, 11 regress.
  • Interpretation: the two representations are complementary. TF-IDF carries lexical identity (brands, amenities, typo-tolerant character n-grams); E5 carries semantic paraphrase and spelled-out numbers.

  • Operator detection is not the bottleneck. Given a field's own span, the cue lexicon gets bounded vs exact right 97–99% of the time. It sees only that field's span text, never the whole query; the cues were developed on train spans only.
  • The gold names do not follow the taxonomy rule. Even with the true operator, the rule "bounded → טווח_*, exact → point" matches only 46% of test twin names and 37% of the hand-written gold rows. Gold annotators mostly use the point name for bounds ("עד 2 מיליון" → מחיר).
  • So strict field-name accuracy is capped by an unresolved labelling convention, not by the model. Applying the taxonomy-consistent rule lowers strict accuracy against current gold (test strict for Exp3: 0.709 with the default names, 0.685 with operators).
  • Decision needed (not a modelling task): either the taxonomy's טווח_* filters are the intended output for bounded values (then gold should be relabelled), or the point fields carry bounds and the טווח_* names should be retired.
  • Mixed-operator queries are almost absent: 0 in val_eval, 2 in test (both detected correctly). The field-local design is the right one ("דירת 4 חדרים עד 2 מיליון"), but this dataset cannot stress-test it. Targeted examples are needed.

7. Confidence features for LLM routing

Per query, the records keep domain_probs, slot_probs, predicted_slots, min_selected_probability, max_rejected_probability and prediction_margin, plus threshold-normalised versions. slot_exact_match is stored as the evaluation target and is never an inference feature.

  • Domain confidence is close to chance (AUROC 0.55–0.58) for "is the whole slot set right?". It cannot drive escalation alone.
  • The raw selected-vs-rejected margin is the best single score, and Exp3's margin ranks best.
  • The threshold-normalised margin ranks worse than the raw one. Per-slot thresholds distort comparability across slots.
  • No router was built. A routing calibrator needs data not used for thresholds (val_calib predictions are in-sample for calibration), e.g. cross-fitted train predictions or a new held-out set.

9. Conclusions and recommendations

  1. Use fusion (Exp3) as the field-presence model when the ~20 ms per-query budget (or ~5 ms batched) is acceptable. It is the most accurate (test slot exact-set 0.733), has the best-calibrated domain head, and has the most useful margin for LLM escalation. Keep Exp2 as the fast path (3.5 ms, no encoder) if latency or footprint dominates; it costs 3.4 points.
  2. Keep the 70-slot space for presence detection (+1.1, significant), and resolve names in the taxonomy layer from a field-local operator once the span/value extractor exists. First, settle the point vs טווח_* convention with the taxonomy owner: current gold contradicts the taxonomy for most bounded values.
  3. Close the gold-row gap (−15 points vs silver): add hand-written or real queries to training, plus typo variants. All curves are still rising.
  4. Routing: build the confidence calibrator on Exp3's raw margin plus slot/domain probabilities, fit on data not used for thresholds. Model P(all slots correct | outputs) now and P(JSON correct | parser outputs) once values are extracted.
  5. Data gaps to fill before trusting the operator path: mixed-operator queries (≈0 today) and twin examples labelled under the agreed convention.
