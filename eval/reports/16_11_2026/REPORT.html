Shipped parser on the test split

Generated from REPORT.md by report_html.py.

Data. eval/dataset/data/test.jsonl: the held-out test split, the same 2,181 rows as split1 test (2,081 searches, 100 non-searches). Labels are in schema v2.

Parser. The parser as shipped in src/artifacts: the Exp7 classifier, the field-local extractor and the confidence gate at threshold 0.831. Nothing was tuned on test; this is the one final check.

Answer.

  • The gate accepted 1,092 of 2,181 queries (50.1%). Of those, 945 (86.5%) came back with the whole JSON exactly right (category, fields and values) and 147 with at least one mistake.
  • That matches the audit: 51.7% accepted at 86.1% correct, certified lower bound 0.822. Test lower bound: 0.848 (counted per row).
  • The other 1,089 queries go to the fallback. 603 of them would have been parsed correctly; the gate gives those up to keep the accepted share accurate.
  • Over all rows, the parser alone gets the category right 98.5% of the time, the field set 76.3% and the whole JSON 75.4%. Category is the easy part.

5. Caveats

  • Test was scored once for this parser. Its numbers must not feed back into thresholds or model choices.
  • The gate's reliability claim comes from a synthetic, LLM-written routing batch; test is also synthetic plus a hand-written gold part. Neither is real traffic.
  • The 86% figure is interim: the target was lowered to let some traffic through while the BERT-style tagger is explored.
